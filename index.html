About Us

A trusted partner for one of life’s biggest decisions

FRNCO Consultants LLC is a real estate consulting company based in Miami Gardens, Florida. We focus on helping individuals and families buy, sell and transfer single-family homes and residential apartment properties.

From the first conversation to the final signature, we explain each step in plain language, so you always understand your options, your risks and your next move.

  • Clear, honest guidance for individual clients
  • Focus on single-family homes and family residences
  • Deep knowledge of the local Florida market
